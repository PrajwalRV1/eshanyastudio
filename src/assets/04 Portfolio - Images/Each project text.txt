01. House of Lattices
Residential Interior Design in Koramangala | Studio Eshanya

House of Lattices is a residential interior design project in Koramangala, Bengaluru, shaped around warmth, natural materials and crafted detail. Lattice-inspired elements bring rhythm and filtered light into the interiors, creating a balance of privacy, openness and everyday comfort.

02.Marabu Veedu
Residential Interior Design in Koramangala | Studio Eshanya

Marabu Veedu is a residential interior design project in Koramangala, Bengaluru, drawing from the warmth and character of traditional Indian homes. Natural materials, crafted details and a sense of familiarity come together to create interiors that feel rooted while remaining comfortable for contemporary living.

03.The Simple Dwelling
Residential Architecture & Interior Design in Chikkabellandur | Studio Eshanya

The Simple Dwelling is a residential architecture and interior design project in Chikkabellandur, Bengaluru, centred around simplicity, natural light and everyday comfort. Clean forms, warm materials and carefully considered interiors create a calm and contemporary home.

04.The Green Corner
Residential Architecture & Interior Design in Varthur | Studio Eshanya

The Green Corner is a residential architecture and interior design project in Varthur, Bengaluru, where greenery becomes an integral part of the interior experience. Warm wood, natural textures and a contemporary palette create a comfortable home with a strong connection to nature.

05.The Pastel Studio
Residential Interior Design in Varthur | Studio Eshanya

The Pastel Studio is a residential interior design project in Varthur, Bengaluru, for a airbnb defined by a warm and expressive interior palette. Soft tones, crafted joinery and carefully layered details bring personality to the home while maintaining a calm and contemporary character.

06.Pushkarini Café
Commercial Interior Design in Davangere | Studio Eshanya

Pushkarini Café is a commercial interior design project in Davangere, Karnataka, designed to create a welcoming and distinctive dining environment. Thoughtful planning, warm materials and a considered visual identity come together to shape an inviting café experience.

07.Bānangala
Residential Architecture & Interior Design in Dharmasthala | Studio Eshanya

Bānangala is an ongoing residential architecture and interior design project in Dharmasthala, Karnataka. Rooted in its setting, the home brings together a familiar architectural character, considered spatial planning and contemporary comfort.

08.House of Layers
Residential Interior Design in Sarjapura | Studio Eshanya

House of Layers is an ongoing residential interior design project in Sarjapura, Bengaluru, exploring a richer and more expressive approach to contemporary living. Layered materials, colour, texture and detailed interiors come together to create a home with a distinct visual character.
